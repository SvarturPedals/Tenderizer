Tenderizer is a recreation of the Gristleizer Eurorack Module once produced by Endangered Audio Research.

I plan to offer board sets at minimal cost for any DIYer who wants them.  
At cost plus 10-15% plus shipping is my goal for boardsets.

On occasion, I may offer built and tested modules on a limited basis through my instagram and/or a future website.
Price for such completed modules TBD at a later date.

Goals and Objectives of this project:
Order Prototype Boards by end of September 2026/Early October 2026
Build prototype boards, troubleshoot if needed, produce final version of boards for release January 1, 2027

File schematic.pdf in repo is original EAR schematic
